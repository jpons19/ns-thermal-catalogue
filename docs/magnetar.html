17 magnetars retained for separate discussion. These entries are outside the passive-cooling sample; thermal values remain catalogue interpretations, with magnetic heating and magnetospheric processing caveats.

How to read the values and colours

Each source has separate age, distance, flux, temperature and magnetic-field assessments. Agreed means agreement within the stated comparison or an accepted reference interpretation; shared assumptions are identified in the explanation. Adopted / revised marks a selected review range or published revision, without implying consensus. Open issue marks unresolved alternatives or conventions. Disputed marks a contested association or identity. Limited means insufficient independent evidence, missing checks, a conditional component or an upper limit.

Flux assesses the thermal spectral component and its bolometric correction, not distance alone. A luminosity change caused only by d² belongs in the distance column; the measurement tables continue to report L∞. Bolometric fluxes have not been newly fitted. A BB versus atmosphere temperature difference can affect both flux and temperature. Adopted uncertainty envelopes can therefore coexist with open spectral issues.

Hover over a cell for its reason, or expand the source to read all five explanations. Grey does not mean agreement. These indicators summarize the existing audit; sources outside the review scope remain limited unless explicitly assessed. Physical class and eligibility for cooling remain separate. Temperatures retain the model, component and redshift conventions of each reference.

Numbers inside the coloured cells: accepted review ranges take precedence; otherwise the named catalogue fit is shown. Age marked τc is a spin-down proxy. Flux is a derived diagnostic in 10⁻¹³ erg cm⁻² s⁻¹ at one stated reference distance; the accompanying L∞ range is in 10³² erg/s. It is not a new flux measurement or a common-confidence interval. Temperature remains fit/component dependent. Bdip is an equatorial canonical dipole estimate in 10¹² G; V13 polar estimates are divided by two. Missing fields remain blank (—). Hover or expand for status and provenance.
